# Bramhatech Invoice Studio

- Product: single-user browser invoice app for Bramhatech Lab, branded for Saikat Chowdhury.
- Design: warm ivory surfaces, black and amber-orange brand accents, restrained serif/sans invoice styling, a faint grid on the A4 invoice, and a live preview.
- Main workflow: choose a saved client or enter client details, add a saved product or custom item, review the live total, optionally use staged milestone payments or disable the schedule for prepaid invoices, add a reusable note, then save a draft or download a PDF.
- Product and client directories are updated from saved drafts and generated PDFs. Company details, signature, products, clients, notes, drafts, and history are local to the browser. Do not imply account sync, email delivery, or server persistence.
- Use `public/bramhatech-wordmark.png` without a tagline and `public/invoice-grid.png` for the faint invoice-paper grid. Allow the owner to replace the logo in Admin Panel.
